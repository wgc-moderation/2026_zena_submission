Use focus sessions to support your study and make it feel more manageable!

The Pomodoro Technique was created by Francesco Cirillo as a way to make work and study feel more manageable. It uses a timer to split work into focused intervals, traditionally 25 minutes long, followed by short breaks. Each study interval is called a pomodoro, named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer Cirillo used as a university student.
